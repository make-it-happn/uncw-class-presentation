02

Lesson Learned

Be careful who you
get into business with

  • Don't go 50/50 with someone you don't fully trust
  • Watch for red flags — if the values don't align, that's a data point
  • I found fake invoices being deposited into a personal account
  • Getting him out was painful. The real question: do they share your values?
03

Team Building

Hire the best.
Values are everything.

  • When the business starts working, hire the absolute best people you can
  • Integrity is table stakes — no exceptions
  • If I sensed dishonesty, I shut it down immediately
  • Practice Radical Candor — care personally, challenge directly

Radical Candor · Kim Scott

The Candor Matrix

Challenge Directly →
Care Personally →

Ruinous Empathy

You care but don't challenge. You're "nice" but not helpful. Problems fester.

Radical Candor

Care personally AND challenge directly. The goal. Kind and clear.

Manipulative Insincerity

Neither caring nor challenging. Passive-aggressive or politically motivated.

Obnoxious Aggression

You challenge but don't care. Brutal honesty without empathy.

04

Scaling

You gotta get
hungry

  • Starting over from scratch after Exit Intel was brutal
  • I was hanging on sales, relying on the business, not putting myself out there
  • The most invaluable experience — doing the sales calls, building partnerships
  • Go talk to 20 customers. It's stereotypical advice because it absolutely works
05

Customer Obsession

Get close to
your customer

  • A little consulting alongside your product isn't a bad thing
  • The product has to scale without you — but knowing what good looks like is an edge
  • Become an expert in helping your customers deliver results
  • Use that expertise to tailor the software for their success

Just Go

You don't need
permission.

  • Register the LLC and charge someone money for something
  • Stripe, Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code — the tools are incredible right now
  • Be unbelievably curious: What is possible? How could I do that?
  • Build something, test it, see if people will pay
06

Be Curious

Learn from
others

Reach out. Most people who've done it will say "grab coffee, shoot me a question." Take them all up on that.

Help Others

Give first

For me it's always been about helping other people be successful. If I can help them succeed, I'll be successful in the process.

07

Level Up

Optimize your
routine

  • How are you leveling up personally? You've got to continually be doing that
  • Is your daily schedule optimized for highest impact?
  • Too much time on social media? Get off of it
  • Invest in real-life relationships — that's where the real returns are

Leadership

Lead the mission.
Learn to sell.

Get people fired up. Set the vision. Inspire them to come along on the journey. And learn how to sell — that's the most important thing you can learn as an entrepreneur.

08

Systems

Business is all
about systems

  • Every business is a function of the systems you set up to scale
  • Three types: the entrepreneur, the manager, and the technician
  • It cannot be about you — build it so it runs without you
  • Once you get some scale, look at EOS for solid foundations

Persistence

It's an absolute
roller coaster

  • You will fail. A lot. Most ideas won't work — that's normal
  • The people who win are the ones crazy enough to keep going
  • Every successful founder has a graveyard of failed attempts behind them
  • Monday you'll feel unstoppable. Tuesday you'll want to quit. Wednesday you get back up
  • Good — keep going

Zero Risk

You will never have
less to lose

  • No mortgage. No kids. No one depending on your paycheck yet
  • Worst case? Move back in with your parents for 6 months
  • It will never be easier to take a swing than right now
  • Once you're in a 9-to-5 with a salary and benefits, the golden handcuffs go on fast
  • The window where you can bet on yourself with almost zero downside is right now — don't waste it

One Last Thing

There's no better
time than right now.

Don't make the excuse of "I'll do it when I'm older." You've got to grind it out. It's going to be super hard. But go build something.

Go make it happen
